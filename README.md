# Project Overview
This project was made to demonstrate which states have experienced the most economic growth and identify which industries have been the major drivers of change. For many states, especially Tennessee, the landscape of our local economies has shifted dramatically over the last decade. To better understand what economic forces have shaped this, the project compares GDP growth with trade volume (both by NAICS Industry) to visualize which factors could have lead to these changes.

## Project Objective
Using data from the U.S. Census Bureau and the Bureau of Economic Analysis, the final dashboard is built as a tool for business decision-makers to understand which industries drive economic development in each state and identify potential areas of investment or industry trends. The analysis seeks to answer the following questions:
- Which states have experienced the strongest overall economic growth?
- Which industries comprise local economies? Which have experienced the most growth?
- How dependent is the local economy on international trade? Has trade exposure increased or decreased?
- Is each state's trade exposure export-driven or import-driven? Which industry groups make up imports and exports?
