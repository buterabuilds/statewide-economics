# Project Overview

This project was created to demonstrate which states have experienced the most economic growth and identify the industries that have been the major drivers of change. For many states, especially Tennessee, the landscape of local economies has shifted dramatically over the last decade. To better understand the economic forces behind these changes, the project compares GDP growth with trade volume—both by NAICS industry—to visualize the factors that may have contributed to them.

## Project Objective

Using data from the U.S. Census Bureau and the Bureau of Economic Analysis, the final dashboard is designed to help business decision-makers understand which industries drive economic development in each state and identify potential investment opportunities and industry trends.

The analysis seeks to answer the following questions:

- Which states have experienced the strongest overall economic growth?
- Which industries comprise local economies, and which have experienced the most growth?
- How dependent is each local economy on international trade? Has trade exposure increased or decreased?
- Is each state’s trade exposure export-driven or import-driven? Which industry groups make up its imports and exports?
